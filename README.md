# Vortex Music

A free, open-source Android music player for selective listeners.
Plays your own music library with a neon-dark design. No account, no ads, no tracking.

> **Status: beta.** The app is currently in Turkish only. English translation is coming.

## Features

**Library**
- Songs, artists, albums, folders, favorites and smart lists
- Add your own cover image to any album
- Group all songs of an artist into one album, or use the album names from your files
- Reorder albums and songs by hand (the order is kept only inside the app, your files are never touched)
- Sorting, layouts (list, compact, grid) and per-list options

**Playback**
- Background playback with notification controls (tapping the notification opens the app)
- Queue screen: reorder, remove, "play next", "add to queue"
- Shuffle, repeat, speed control (with optional pitch-linked "vinyl" mode)
- Smooth fade-in, remembers your last queue and position
- Cover-based colors on the now-playing screen

**Sound**
- Equalizer, bass boost, loudness and virtual surround
- Save your settings as profiles, export and import them as files
- Automatic profile switching when a Bluetooth or wired headphone connects

**Search**
- Fast search that ignores case and Turkish letters ("sezen" finds "Şezen")
- Search by song, artist or album, with sort options and recent searches

**Your listening**
- Statistics: time per song and artist, daily and hourly charts (kept on your phone)
- Smart lists: most played, recently added, never played, forgotten songs

**Tools**
- Lyrics: import `.lrc` / `.txt` files or paste them; synced lyrics highlight as the song plays
- Duplicate file finder with safe deletion (Android 11+)
- Appearance: accent colors, optional neon lines, true-black mode
- Backup and restore of your favorites, folders, lyrics, covers and settings

## Privacy

Everything stays on your phone. The app does not collect data, show ads or require an account.

## Known issues

- The interface is Turkish only for now.
- Equalizer strength depends on your phone brand; some devices weaken Android's built-in audio effects.
- Backups are meant for restoring on the same phone.
